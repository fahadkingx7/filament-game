FILE / 001

THE BOY

The protagonist died at 18 after years of severe neglect in his foster family's room. He travels without realizing he is dead; the world he passes through is deliberately unexplained.

FILE / 002

BROTHER

The elder brother he remembers never physically existed. He was the imagined protector through whom the boy carried his own resilience, courage and self-compassion.

FILE / 003

THE FINAL CONFRONTATION

Brother becomes the final boss — The One Who Kept You Alive. The climactic revelation reframes remembered rescues as the boy's own acts of survival. The choreography and exact True Ending remain undecided.

FILE / 004

LUNA'S BOAT ENDING

Luna, coerced by the independent organization The Anchors, brings him to a larger ship where he is captured. Her younger sister meets her there. The sisters leave as the city disappears into fog.

The game's supernatural origin, the protagonist's final fate, and several other questions remain deliberately unanswered or in development.
