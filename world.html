01 / THE METROPOLIS CITY RECORDS

A city that
refuses to die.

The architecture is an unlikely harmony: Venetian waterways, Italian stone, Japanese and French influences, and immense analog infrastructure built over the wounds of a war that ran from the 1910s to the 1950s.

Airships drift past skybridges. Pod-metro lines disappear into cloud. Streets built for people now feel almost too quiet.
